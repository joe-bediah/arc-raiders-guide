Field Guide

Threat profiles, weak points, and engagement tactics for every ARC machine encountered on Solace. Study before you drop.

// Guide Version: Patch 1.29.0 — May 19, 2026
This guide reflects the current live game state as of the Nomadic Envoy update (1.29.0). Major enemy changes introduced in Riven Tides (1.26.0) and Flashpoint (1.22.0) are incorporated throughout. Entries with a ⚠ PATCH NOTE badge were specifically updated in a recent patch.

⚠ Note: Embark Studios patches frequently. Individual cards note their last-relevant patch where applicable. Always check arcraiders.com/news after major updates.

Threat: Low
Small Threats
Tick
HP ~10–50
Attack Pattern
Lurks motionless on walls, ceilings, and in dark corners indoors. When a raider passes close it leaps directly at the head, latches on, and deals continuous damage before detonating. Frequently found in clusters. As of 1.29.0, players can drop a carryable item to break the latch and shake one off.
Weak Points
Entire body (extremely fragile)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Raider Tool (silent 1-hit melee) Any pistol / light ammo Flashlight (spot before it leaps) Carryable item (drop to break latch — patch 1.29.0)
Strategy: A single melee hit is an instant, silent kill — the Raider Tool is ideal. Scan ceilings and corners with your flashlight before entering any building. Kill them before they leap; if one latches on, dropping a carryable item now breaks the hold (added in 1.29.0). Never fire a gun at one indoors if stealth matters — it alerts everything nearby.
Pop
HP ~20–40
Attack Pattern
Small suicide drone. Rolls rapidly toward a target emitting an accelerating high-pitched beep. Detonates on contact — but if you destroy it before it completes its self-detonation sequence, the blast is harmless. The full self-detonation is the dangerous version.
Weak Points
Entire body (unarmored)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Raider Tool (melee — 1 hit kill) Any firearm
Strategy: The moment you hear the beep, intercept it — one melee strike destroys it safely before it can arm. Do not let it reach you. In groups, they often roll in quick succession; prioritize by sound. Keep moving so multiple Pops can't converge simultaneously.
Fireball
HP ~20–100
Attack Pattern
Armored rolling bot. Closes distance, pauses, then opens its front chassis panels and blasts a cone of fire. Front armor is normally closed and highly resistant — the vulnerable core is only exposed during the fire attack. Explodes violently on death — maintain distance.
Weak Points
Exposed core (during fire attack only)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Medium/Heavy ammo rifle Shotgun (burst at core during opening) Keep 5m+ distance — death explosion is wide
Strategy: Bait its fire attack from a safe distance, then punish the exposed core as it opens. Sidestep its flame arc — it can't track well mid-attack. Back off immediately after firing; the death explosion has a large radius. Never melee this enemy.
Threat: Medium
Medium Threats
Turret
HP ~80–200
Attack Pattern
Stationary indoor gun emplacement. Scans with a blue laser. Switches to red on detection and immediately opens rapid fire. High rate of fire will deplete shields quickly at close range. Placement at chokepoints and corridors amplifies the threat. As of 1.26.0, additional Sentinel spawn points were added — watch elevated positions near turret clusters.
Weak Points
Barrel / sensor head Base (melee blind spot)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Raider Tool (silent melee from below) Any firearm from cover Smoke Grenade (break line of sight)
Strategy: A single melee strike to the base is completely silent. From range, use cover and peek-shoot its sensor. Smoke grenades buy time to close for a melee finish. Never rush through a doorway without scanning for the blue laser first.
Snitch / Spotter
HP ~80–158
Attack Pattern
Scout drone that flies at altitude scanning for players. Deals zero direct damage — its entire threat is detecting you and broadcasting your position to every ARC in the area, triggering a swarm response. Will flee when threatened. The Bombardier spawns Spotters that respawn every 20–30s while the Bombardier is alive.
Weak Points
Thrusters Scanner sensor (body shots)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Semi-auto rifle (precise, quiet) Suppressed weapon preferred
Strategy: Kill on sight — always. A Snitch that escapes turns every nearby ARC hostile. Prioritize it over everything else in a mixed group. If it has already scanned you, break line of sight with buildings immediately and expect a swarm.
Wasp
HP ~80–110
Attack Pattern
The most common ARC. Basic flying drone with a rapid-fire gun that deals steady chip damage. Moves in arcing patterns to make itself harder to hit. Usually in groups. Individually weak but dangerous alongside heavier enemies that demand your attention.
Weak Points
Thrusters (underside)
Recommended Weapons & Items
SMG / Light ammo Semi-auto pistol Frag Grenade (pack hunting)
Strategy: Low HP — burst fire to the body is enough at close range. Target the thrusters for fastest kills. When multiple Wasps are present, use a grenade to hit the cluster. Don't ignore them while fighting larger enemies; their combined chip damage drains you faster than you expect.
Hornet
HP ~200–300
Attack Pattern
Lightly armored flying drone. Closes aggressively and delivers electric stun rounds that briefly stagger you. Being stunned in a multi-enemy fight is usually fatal. More durable and faster than a Wasp.
Weak Points
Rear thrusters
Recommended Weapons & Items
Medium ammo AR / rifle Shotgun (close range) EMP Grenade (disables stun ability)
Strategy: Prioritize Hornets over Wasps in mixed groups — their stun is the greater threat. Target rear thrusters for a damage multiplier. EMP grenades temporarily disable their electric attack. Stay mobile; a stationary target is much easier to stun.
Shredder
HP ~480–600
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.22.0
Attack Pattern
Bulky aerial platform. Originally confined to Stella Montis, Shredders were expanded to all maps in the Flashpoint update (1.22.0). On Dam Battlegrounds they only appear during certain map conditions. Charges up a massive shrapnel blast that covers a wide arc — the charge-up is telegraphed with a brief hover and spinning front weapons. During the blast it's focused on firing and can't track fast-moving targets well.
Weak Points
Thrusters (underside / rear — blue units)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Shotgun (close burst at thrusters) Heavy ammo rifle Canto SMG (added 1.22.0 — natural counter per Embark) Frag / EMP Grenade Hard cover (walls, rock formations)
Strategy: Wait for the charge-up animation, then immediately get behind hard cover. Let it complete its shrapnel blast, then step out and unload into the thrusters. Repeat: cover during attack, fire during cooldown. Hitting the thrusters staggers the unit and opens follow-up damage windows. Do not fight in the open — its shrapnel spread is lethal at range. Note: as of 1.29.0, Shredders are reported to float to the ceiling in some rooms of the Hidden Bunker (known bug).
Firefly
HP ~200
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.29.0
Attack Pattern
Aerial drone that spews fire downward. Added in Shrouded Sky (1.17.0). As of 1.29.0, fire application was improved to match visuals more closely — briefly grazing the flame is no longer as punishing, reducing the "hit around corners" issue. Firefly spawn rates were significantly reduced in 1.22.0 (Flashpoint) and Firefly presence in Dam Battlegrounds was further reduced in 1.26.0 (Riven Tides). Particularly dangerous during Hurricane map conditions.
Weak Points
Fuel tank (rear/underside) Thrusters
Recommended Weapons & Items
Semi-auto rifle (fuel tank shots) Shotgun (if it drops low) EMP Grenade (interrupts burn attack)
Strategy: Keep moving — its downward fire spray can't track a fast-moving ground target effectively. Aim for the fuel tank under its body for bonus damage. During Hurricane conditions, listen for its jet-engine audio cue. The 1.29.0 fire fix means grazing the edge of its flame is no longer an insta-punish — use that to stay more aggressive in close quarters.
Comet
HP ~150
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.26.0
Attack Pattern
Heavily armored ground unit added in Shrouded Sky (1.17.0). Charges at full speed on detection, then splits its armor to reveal an explosive core and detonates in a devastating seismic explosion. In 1.26.0 (Riven Tides), Comets were removed from standard Dam Battlegrounds sessions and their presence in Buried City was reduced, as they were raising difficulty in beginner sessions too high.
Weak Points
Core (only exposed at point of detonation)
Recommended Weapons & Items
High-damage single shot (rifle, shotgun) EMP Grenade (stalls its charge) Height advantage (ledge / rooftop)
Strategy: Run perpendicular to its charge rather than straight back — it has a fixed charge path. Stall it with an EMP then fire heavy rounds into the exposed core. Do not hide behind cover for the detonation — the seismic shockwave goes through surfaces. Best position is height: get on a ledge or rooftop it can't reach before it detonates.
Surveyor
HP ~257–400
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.26.0
Attack Pattern
Large spherical ARC that rolls patrol routes. Stops to fire a precision laser at detected players. Heavily armored while rolling — almost impenetrable in ball form from all angles. Opens a panel to fire the laser, briefly exposing its core. Also opens when transmitting data. In 1.26.0, a bug was fixed where Vaporizer and Surveyor patrols could stop moving until alerted — they now patrol correctly.
Weak Points
Core (exposed during laser fire / transmit only)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Heavy ammo rifle (core shots) Shotgun burst on exposed core EMP Grenade (interrupts attack cycle)
Strategy: Don't waste ammo on its rolling shell. Wait for it to stop and open up for the laser or a data transmission, then dump the heaviest weapon you have into the core. Use cover to avoid the laser while it tracks. EMPs interrupt the attack cycle and buy an extra damage window. The vault it carries makes the patience worthwhile.
Rollbot
HP ~800
Attack Pattern
Fully plated ball-shaped ARC that rolls at high speed with the sole intent of running you over. No shooting mode — pure kinetic aggression. Heavily armored from every angle in ball form. Getting hit results in significant knockback and damage. Can chain roll-overs if you're trapped in a corridor.
Weak Points
Core seam (brief window between rolls)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Heavy ammo — sustained fire on seam EMP Grenade (stalls roll, creates window) High ground (limits roll paths)
Strategy: Never fight in a straight corridor — it will run you down. Fight in open areas where you can sidestep its charge. EMP grenades stop the roll and create a damage window. Dump heavy ammo into the seam between charges. Getting to higher ground removes most of its threat entirely.
Sentinel
HP ~300–500
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.26.0
Attack Pattern
Heavy stationary sniper turret on rooftops and elevated positions. Fires devastating high-velocity rounds that chunk a major portion of health in one hit. The yellow canister on its arm glows on lock-on — that's your warning. Additional Sentinel spawn points were added in 1.22.0 (Flashpoint) with more added and spawns made more consistent in 1.26.0. Also, aim assist now correctly targets Sentinel leg joints and weak spots as of 1.26.0.
Weak Points
Yellow core canister (arm)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Scoped rifle (reach the core at range) Heavy ammo AR with scope Smoke Grenade (break line of sight)
Strategy: Flanking is the safest approach — get out of its sightline and approach from the side or below its elevation. Smoke grenades cut its targeting immediately. In direct engagement, use hard cover and only peek to fire at the yellow canister. One or two precise hits deal massive damage. Never stand still in its sightline.
Threat: High / Critical
Large Threats
Bombardier
HP ~2,670–3,000
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.26.0
Attack Pattern
Tall siege-class ARC that rains mortar rounds in area-denial barrages. Extremely dangerous in open terrain. Also capable of a kinetic shockwave energy release at closer range. Spawns Spotter drones that respawn every 20–30s while it lives. In 1.26.0, Bombardier area coverage was adjusted to improve navigation. Aim assist now correctly targets Bombardier leg joints as of 1.26.0.
Weak Points
Yellow leg joints Rear canister
Recommended Weapons & Items
Heavy ammo AR / LMG Rocket launcher / Rascal (rear canister) Smoke Grenade (disrupt mortar targeting) EMP Grenade (halt shockwave)
Strategy: Kill Spotter drones first — they keep it informed of your position. Use buildings as mortar cover; keep moving between shots. Target the glowing yellow leg joints while it fires, then advance to hit the rear canister. Smoke breaks mortar targeting effectively. Treat this as a sustained fight requiring cover discipline, not a stand-and-gun encounter.
Bastion
HP ~2,050–5,000
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.26.0
Attack Pattern
Four-legged walking tank with a heavy turret on top. Frontal armor is exceptionally thick — direct frontal engagement is highly inefficient. Fires a sustained projectile volley. Slow but deliberate; it rotates to face you. In 1.26.0, a bug was fixed where the Bastion could be too accurate when switching targets mid-fire — it now behaves more fairly in multi-target situations. Aim assist now correctly targets leg joints and weak spots.
Weak Points
Rear armor plate Leg joints
Recommended Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher / Rascal (rear plate) Heavy ammo AR / LMG (leg joints) Lure / Decoy (pull attention for flanking) EMP Grenade (brief stagger)
Strategy: The rear plate and leg joints are where meaningful damage lands — circling to its back is the core tactic. In a squad, split into two groups: one holds frontal aggro while the other flanks to the rear. A lure or decoy is excellent for making it rotate away. Its slow turning speed is your greatest advantage.
Leaper
HP ~1,100–3,500
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.26.0
Attack Pattern
Assault-class ARC (also called Bison). Leaps extraordinary distances, instantly closing any range advantage. On landing it releases a kinetic shockwave that staggers anyone nearby. Makes cover and ranged positioning much less effective. In 1.26.0, Leaper area coverage was adjusted for improved navigation and variety. Leaper distribution during Night Raid conditions was also increased in 1.26.0. As of 1.29.0, the Leaper can jump through the ARC Turbine (known bug).
Weak Points
Leg joints Eye sensor
Recommended Weapons & Items
Heavy ammo AR (sustained leg joint fire) Shotgun (if it closes distance) EMP Grenade (interrupt leap mid-jump) Explosive Grenade (shockwave counter)
Strategy: Do not rely on range — this enemy erases distance. Accept the close engagement and target leg joints consistently. An EMP thrown as it winds up a leap can interrupt it entirely. When it lands near you, immediately roll sideways to avoid the shockwave AoE. Fight in enclosed areas where its leap range is limited by geometry.
Rocketeer
HP ~1,100–4,000
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.29.0
Attack Pattern
Large aerial heavy platform that hovers at altitude and rains rockets onto player positions. Its rockets are area-denial — they punish static cover hard. As of 1.22.0 (Flashpoint), Rocketeers take less collision damage and won't be instantly destroyed on impact when stunned. As of 1.29.0, when the Rocketeer fires several missiles at once, they are now spread over a larger area — preventing the unfair situation of multiple missiles hitting the same spot simultaneously.
Weak Points
Thrusters (require sustained damage to destroy)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher / Rascal grenade launcher Heavy ammo AR or LMG EMP Grenade (interrupt rockets, drop altitude)
Strategy: Never stay in one spot — its rockets will blanket your position. Keep moving laterally between cover pieces. The 1.29.0 missile spread fix means multi-missile salvos are now more distributed — slightly safer to reposition through. Fire rockets or heavy rounds into its thrusters while it's mid-attack cycle. EMP can interrupt a rocket salvo and drop it lower for easier shots.
Vaporizer
HP ~1,500–8,000
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.26.0
Attack Pattern
Added in Flashpoint (1.22.0). Large aerial ARC with a Matriarch-grade energy shield and precision laser attacks that destroy your shield and ignite you in under two seconds. Patrols ARC Operations and Close Scrutiny zones. In 1.26.0, Vaporizers were added to more maps and conditions beyond the original Close Scrutiny event. A movement bug where Vaporizer patrols could stop until alerted was also fixed in 1.26.0. The energy shield blocks all standard projectiles while active.
Weak Points
Thrusters (when shield is down only)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher (burst during shield break) Heavy ammo AR (thruster fire) EMP Grenade — CRITICAL (breaks energy shield) Fire-resistant trinket / armor
Strategy: EMP grenades are mandatory — they break the energy shield. The moment the shield drops, dump everything into the thrusters. You have a short window before it reactivates. Never fight in the open; the laser will kill you before you can react. Stay mobile and use building angles to break its laser tracking. Fire-resistant armor dramatically extends your survival time if it lands a beam. Killing one drops the Vaporizer Regulator needed to craft the Dolabra energy shotgun.
Threat: Extreme
Boss Encounters

// These encounters require coordinated squad play. Solo engagement is strongly discouraged.

The Queen
HP ~15,000–33,864
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.29.0
Attack Pattern
Apex-class boss (Harvester event). Gigantic robot with heavy mortar fire, a precision laser beam, an area EMP pulse, and a ground slam at close range. Her missiles can obliterate an unprepared squad instantly. As of 1.29.0, the Queen (and Matriarch) will now more often step over obstacles rather than through them, reducing instances of unfair player knockback. Aim assist now correctly targets leg joints and weak spots as of 1.26.0.
Weak Points
Leg joints Head sensors Core (exposed in later phases)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher / Rascal (leg joints / core) Heavy ammo LMG (sustained leg fire) Full squad of 3 — split aggro EMP Grenades (interrupt laser / EMP pulse) Shield cells / med kits — bring plenty
Strategy: Split the squad on opposite sides to split her aggro — she cannot fire effectively at both simultaneously. Designate leg joints as primary focus and all fire together to strip sections. When she charges the EMP pulse, take cover and shield up immediately. Avoid melee range entirely — the ground slam is a near-instant kill. The 1.29.0 collision fix reduces instances where her movement through obstacles causes unfair knockback. Communicate phase transitions — she becomes more aggressive as HP drops.
Matriarch
HP ~20,000+
⚠ Updated: Patch 1.29.0
Attack Pattern
The deadliest boss in the game. Spawns on Dam Battlegrounds, Blue Gate, and Spaceport only — never on Stella Montis, Buried City, or Riven Tides. Projects an energy shield blocking all projectiles when active. Fires rocket swarms, deploys gas mortars, uses flashbangs, and summons ARC reinforcements throughout the fight. As of 1.29.0, she now more often steps over obstacles rather than through them, reducing unfair knockback. Aim assist correctly targets leg joints and weak spots as of 1.26.0.
Weak Points
Leg joints Head core (shield must be down)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher / Rascal (head core during shield break) Heavy ammo AR (leg joint sustained fire) EMP Grenade — CRITICAL (breaks energy shield) Gas Mask / Respirator (counters gas mortars) Assign one player exclusively to adds Flashbang-resistant trinket
Strategy: Assign one squad member exclusively to dealing with summoned ARC adds — letting them build up is a wipe condition. Use EMPs to break the energy shield, then immediately dump rockets or heavy fire into the head core. Gas mortars force movement; equip a gas mask or keep moving through the cloud. Flashbang-resistant trinkets are essential — being blinded during an add wave is extremely dangerous. This is the highest coordination fight in the game.
ARC Turbine
HP ~4,000
⚠ Added: Patch 1.26.0 · Fixed: 1.29.0
Attack Pattern
Airborne boss added in Riven Tides (1.26.0), exclusive to the Riven Tides map. Massive cone-shaped aerial machine that operates in two distinct phases. In its flying phase it is nearly invulnerable — do not waste ammo. It launches a spike into the air that splits into 3 projectiles, creating lightning arcs that penetrate cover and walls. It also deploys mines across a wide perimeter. Spawns after 15 minutes into a match, and can spawn in duos or trios. In its ground phase, it lands on three legs and opens its core — exposing three spinning yellow canisters. You have roughly 4–5 shots worth of time before it lifts off. In 1.29.0, several ARC Turbine visual and positioning bugs were fixed, and the odds of getting burned when inside it were increased.
Weak Points
Three yellow spinning canisters in landing gear (ground phase only)
Recommended Weapons & Items
Hullcracker (strong vs canisters per community) Jupiter / Anvil (multi-round, no reload mid-window) Renegade rifle (precise mid-range canister shots) Medium or Heavy Shield (essential for survival) Shield rechargers + med kits — bring plenty Do NOT bring Wolfpack — ineffective vs Turbine armor
Strategy: Do not fire while it's airborne — you'll waste ammo and trigger its attacks. Use the flying phase to reposition, avoid mines, and move into cover near where it will land. The Turbine tends to land when it has not been attacked for a while — patience triggers the ground phase. The moment it sets down, all squad members fire simultaneously into the spinning yellow canisters through the landing gear slits. You have a narrow window of 4–5 shots before it lifts off again. After taking off, it launches split-lightning grenades that penetrate walls — do not shelter inside buildings expecting cover. Plan your squad positions before it lands so everyone is in range the instant the canisters are exposed. Killing it drops Turbine Compressors needed for the Avian Alarm project and to craft the Powered Descender.
