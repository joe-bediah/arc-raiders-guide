Container Types

Locations by Map
Blue Gate BEST
Underground Car Park (Checkpoint / Underpass)30+ lootable vehicles in a compact loop. Highest car density of any map.
RouteEnter via Checkpoint southwest entrance → descend to underground Underpass → breach every car hood, engine bay, trunk → extract at Checkpoint or nearest surface exit. ~10 min loop. Watch for Bastions on patrol.
Dam Battlegrounds GOOD
North Complex — buses near Extraction Elevatortwo buses with near-guaranteed spawn, widely reported by community.
Controlled Access Zone (between The Breach and Power Generation Complex) — Breachable Cabinets, high drop rate. Added post-Shrouded Sky. Unlock side room with a Fuel Cell for Desk Drawers that also yield Gears.
TipThe buses are fast — hit them first, grab the Elevator extract, done. Avoid looting random cars; buses have much better rates.
Buried City GOOD
Warehouse + Marano Station + Parking Garage (northwest) — cars + generators. Hit Marano car park early before other raiders.
Railroad Parking Lotoutside the railroad, pry open car hoods.
TipGo at Night for less competition. Metro station extract is close to these zones. Breach generators — better rates than regular cars.
Spaceport GOOD
Rocket Assembly + Vehicle Maintenance areasindustrial containers and vehicle hoods.
Stella Montis HIGH RISK
Loading Baygreen breachable containers. Recommended only if already farming here for other reasons.
WarningStella Montis has heavy ARC presence. Not worth the run just for Rusted Gears — Blue Gate is safer and better yield.

ARC Alloy ARC Drop · Workshop upgrades & Expedition · All maps
Any Map
Source
All ARC enemies (every kill) ARC Wreckages (looting destroyed ARC) ARC Probes / Couriers
Best Farming

ARC Alloy drops from every ARC you destroy. The most efficient farming is clearing ARC patrols on whichever map you're already running. Looting ARC Wreckages (destroyed enemy bodies) gives a bonus yield. Probes and Couriers are lootable without fighting.

Standard ARC patrols throughout. Good volume without extreme danger.

Drops alongside ARC Alloy from every ARC kill. Target Wasps and Hornets for fast kills with reliable drops. Also commonly found in ARC Probes which don't require combat.

ARC Circuitry ARC Drop (Rare) · Workshop upgrades · Boss & Elite drops
BEST: Stella Montis
Source
Surveyors (primary drop) Boss-tier ARC (Bastions, Queens) ARC Probes
Best Farming

Farm Surveyors — they reliably drop ARC Circuitry and carry a Surveyor Vault. Dam Battlegrounds Industrial Zone has Surveyor patrols at lower risk than Stella Montis. Electromagnetic Storm events on Dam and Stella Montis increase boss ARC spawns, improving drop rates.
