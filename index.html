Tick HP ~10–50 ⚠ 1.29.0
Body (fragile)
Attack Pattern

Lurks motionless on walls, ceilings, and dark corners indoors. Leaps at your head when you pass close, latches on, and deals continuous damage before detonating. Found in clusters. As of 1.29.0, dropping a carryable item breaks the latch.

Weak Points
Entire body (extremely fragile)
Weapons & Items
Raider Tool — silent 1-hit kill Any pistol / light ammo Flashlight (spot before it leaps) Carryable item (drop to break latch)
Strategy One melee hit kills it silently — use the Raider Tool. Scan ceilings and corners with your flashlight before entering any building. Kill before it leaps; if one latches on, drop a carryable to break the hold. Never fire a gun at one indoors if stealth matters — it alerts everything nearby.

Pop HP ~20–40
Body (unarmored)
Attack Pattern

Small suicide drone. Rolls rapidly toward you emitting an accelerating beep. Detonates on contact. If you destroy it before it completes its self-detonation sequence, the blast is harmless.

Weak Points
Entire body (unarmored)
Weapons & Items
Raider Tool — 1 hit, stops detonation Any firearm
Strategy The moment you hear the beep, intercept it — one melee strike destroys it safely before it can arm. Keep moving so multiple Pops can't converge simultaneously.
Fireball HP ~20–100
Core (fire only)
Attack Pattern

Armored rolling bot. Closes distance, pauses, opens its front chassis panels and blasts a cone of fire. Vulnerable core only exposed during the fire attack. Explodes violently on death — maintain distance.

Weak Points
Exposed core (during fire attack only)
Weapons & Items
Medium/Heavy ammo rifle Shotgun (burst at core during opening) Stay 5m+ away — death explosion is wide
Strategy Bait its fire attack from a safe distance, then punish the exposed core as it opens. Sidestep the flame arc — it can't track mid-attack. Back off immediately after firing. Never melee this enemy.
Medium Medium Threats
Turret HP ~80–200
Barrel / Base
Attack Pattern

Stationary indoor gun emplacement. Scans with a blue laser; switches to red on detection and opens rapid fire. High rate of fire depletes shields quickly. Deadly at chokepoints and corridors. More Sentinel spawn points added near turret clusters in 1.26.0.

Weak Points
Barrel / sensor head Base (melee blind spot)
Weapons & Items
Raider Tool — silent melee from below Any firearm from cover Smoke Grenade (break line of sight)
Strategy Single melee strike to the base is completely silent and instant. From range, use cover and peek-shoot the sensor. Smoke buys time to close for a melee finish. Never rush through a doorway without scanning for the blue laser first.
Snitch / Spotter HP ~80–158
Thrusters
Attack Pattern

Scout drone at altitude. Deals zero direct damage — its threat is broadcasting your position to every ARC nearby, triggering a swarm. Flees when threatened. Bombardiers spawn Spotters that respawn every 20–30s while it lives.

Weak Points
Thrusters Scanner sensor (body shots)
Weapons & Items
Semi-auto rifle (precise, quiet) Suppressed weapon preferred
Strategy Kill on sight — always. A Snitch that escapes turns every nearby ARC hostile. Prioritize it over everything else in a mixed group. If it already scanned you, break line of sight with buildings immediately and brace for a swarm.
Wasp HP ~80–110
Thrusters
Attack Pattern

Most common ARC. Basic flying drone with a rapid-fire gun dealing steady chip damage. Moves in arcing patterns. Usually in groups. Individually weak but dangerous when you're occupied with heavier enemies.

Weak Points
Thrusters (underside)
Weapons & Items
SMG / Light ammo Semi-auto pistol Frag Grenade (hits cluster)
Strategy Low HP — burst fire to the body is enough. Target thrusters for fastest kills. Grenade the cluster when multiple are present. Don't ignore them during big fights; their chip damage adds up fast.
Hornet HP ~200–300
Rear thrusters
Attack Pattern

Lightly armored flying drone. Closes aggressively and delivers electric stun rounds that briefly stagger you. Being stunned in a multi-enemy fight is usually fatal.

Weak Points
Rear thrusters
Weapons & Items
Medium ammo AR / rifle Shotgun (close range) EMP Grenade (disables stun)
Strategy Prioritize Hornets over Wasps in mixed groups — the stun is the bigger threat. Target rear thrusters for a damage multiplier. EMP grenades temporarily disable their electric attack. Stay mobile.
Shredder HP ~480–600 ⚠ 1.22.0 — All maps
Thrusters
Attack Pattern

Bulky aerial platform. Originally Stella Montis only — expanded to all maps in Flashpoint (1.22.0). On Dam Battlegrounds only appears during certain map conditions. Charges up a massive shrapnel blast telegraphed by a brief hover and spinning front weapons. During the blast it can't track fast-moving targets well. Known bug (1.29.0): floats to ceiling in some Hidden Bunker rooms.

Weak Points
Thrusters (underside / rear — blue units)
Weapons & Items
Shotgun (burst at thrusters) Heavy ammo rifle Canto SMG (natural counter per Embark) Frag / EMP Grenade Hard cover — walls, rock formations
Strategy Wait for the charge-up, get behind hard cover, let it complete the shrapnel blast. Step out and unload into the thrusters. Repeat — cover during attack, fire during cooldown. Hitting the thrusters staggers it and opens follow-up windows. Do not fight in the open.
Firefly HP ~200 ⚠ Fire fix 1.29.0
Fuel tank
Attack Pattern

Aerial drone that spews fire downward. Added in Shrouded Sky (1.17.0). Fire application improved in 1.29.0 — grazing the flame edge is no longer as punishing, fixing the "hit around corners" feel. Spawn rates reduced in 1.22.0; Dam Battlegrounds presence further reduced in 1.26.0. Especially dangerous during Hurricane map conditions.

Weak Points
Fuel tank (rear/underside) Thrusters
Weapons & Items
Semi-auto rifle (fuel tank shots) Shotgun (if it drops low) EMP Grenade (interrupts burn attack)
Strategy Keep moving — its downward spray can't track a fast-moving ground target well. Aim for the fuel tank for bonus damage. During Hurricane, listen for its jet-engine audio cue before you see it. The 1.29.0 fire fix means you can be slightly more aggressive at the edge of its flame range.
Comet HP ~150 ⚠ Spawns reduced 1.26.0
Core (detonation)
Attack Pattern

Heavily armored ground unit (Shrouded Sky, 1.17.0). Charges at full speed, splits armor to reveal an explosive core, then detonates with a seismic explosion that goes through surfaces. Removed from standard Dam Battlegrounds sessions in 1.26.0; presence reduced in Buried City.

Weak Points
Core (only exposed at point of detonation)
Weapons & Items
High-damage single shot (rifle, shotgun) EMP Grenade (stalls charge) Height advantage — ledge or rooftop
Strategy Run perpendicular to its charge, not straight back — it has a fixed charge path. Stall it with an EMP then fire into the exposed core. Do not hide behind cover for the detonation — the seismic shockwave goes through surfaces. Best position is height.
Surveyor HP ~257–400 ⚠ Patrol fix 1.26.0
Core (laser/transmit)
Attack Pattern

Large spherical ARC that rolls patrol routes and fires a precision laser. Nearly impenetrable while rolling. Opens a panel to fire, briefly exposing its core. Also opens when transmitting data while stationary. A patrol movement bug (stopped until alerted) was fixed in 1.26.0.

Weak Points
Core (exposed during laser fire / data transmit only)
Weapons & Items
Heavy ammo rifle (core shots) Shotgun burst on exposed core EMP Grenade (interrupts attack cycle)
Strategy Don't waste ammo on its rolling shell. Wait for it to stop and open up, then dump the heaviest weapon into the core. Use cover to avoid the laser while it tracks. EMPs create an extra window. The vault it drops is worth the patience.
Rollbot HP ~800
Core seam
Attack Pattern

Fully plated ball that rolls at high speed to crush you. No shooting mode — pure kinetic aggression. Heavily armored from all angles while rolling. Can chain roll-overs if you're trapped in a corridor.

Weak Points
Core seam (brief window between rolls)
Weapons & Items
Heavy ammo — sustained fire on seam EMP Grenade (stops roll, creates window) High ground (limits roll paths)
Strategy Never fight in a straight corridor. Use open areas to sidestep its charge. EMPs stop the roll and create a damage window. Dump heavy ammo into the seam between charges. High ground removes most of its threat.
Sentinel HP ~300–500 ⚠ More spawns 1.26.0
Yellow canister
Attack Pattern

Heavy stationary sniper turret on rooftops and elevated positions. Fires high-velocity rounds that chunk a large portion of health in one hit. Yellow canister on its arm glows on lock-on — that's your warning. More spawn points added and made more consistent in 1.26.0. Aim assist now correctly targets weak spots as of 1.26.0.

Weak Points
Yellow core canister (arm)
Weapons & Items
Scoped rifle (reach core at range) Heavy ammo AR with scope Smoke Grenade (break line of sight)
Strategy Flanking is safest — get out of its sightline and approach from the side or below. Smoke cuts its targeting immediately. In direct engagement, only peek from hard cover to fire at the yellow canister. Never stand still in its sightline.
Critical Large Threats
Bombardier HP ~2,670–3,000 ⚠ Coverage adj. 1.26.0
Leg joints / Rear
Attack Pattern

Tall siege-class ARC that rains mortar barrages and releases kinetic shockwaves at closer range. Extremely dangerous in open terrain. Spawns Spotter drones that respawn every 20–30s while it lives. Area coverage adjusted for better navigation in 1.26.0; aim assist now targets leg joints correctly.

Weak Points
Yellow leg joints Rear canister
Weapons & Items
Heavy ammo AR / LMG Rocket launcher / Rascal (rear canister) Smoke Grenade (disrupts mortar targeting) EMP Grenade (halts shockwave)
Strategy Kill Spotter drones first — they keep it informed of your position. Use buildings as mortar cover, keep moving. Target the yellow leg joints while it fires, then advance to the rear canister. Smoke breaks mortar targeting. Sustained cover-discipline fight, not a stand-and-gun.
Bastion HP ~2,050–5,000 ⚠ Accuracy fix 1.26.0
Rear plate / Legs
Attack Pattern

Four-legged walking tank with a heavy turret on top. Frontal armor is extremely thick — direct frontal engagement is inefficient. Fires a sustained projectile volley. Slow but deliberate; it rotates to face you. A bug where it was too accurate when switching targets mid-fire was fixed in 1.26.0.

Weak Points
Rear armor plate Leg joints
Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher / Rascal (rear plate) Heavy ammo AR / LMG (leg joints) Lure / Decoy (pull aggro for flank) EMP Grenade (brief stagger)
Strategy Circle to its back — the rear plate and leg joints are where real damage lands. In a squad, split: one holds frontal aggro while the other flanks. A lure is excellent for making it rotate away. Its slow turning speed is your greatest advantage.
Leaper HP ~1,100–3,500 ⚠ Coverage adj. 1.26.0
Leg joints / Eye
Attack Pattern

Assault-class ARC (also: Bison). Leaps extraordinary distances, instantly erasing any range advantage. On landing it releases a kinetic shockwave that staggers nearby players. Area coverage and Night Raid distribution adjusted in 1.26.0. Known bug (1.29.0): can jump through the ARC Turbine.

Weak Points
Leg joints Eye sensor
Weapons & Items
Heavy ammo AR (sustained leg fire) Shotgun (after it closes distance) EMP Grenade (interrupt mid-leap)
Strategy Don't rely on range — it will close instantly. Accept the close engagement, target leg joints consistently. EMP thrown as it winds up a leap can interrupt it entirely. When it lands near you, roll sideways immediately to dodge the shockwave AoE.
Rocketeer HP ~1,100–4,000 ⚠ Missile spread fix 1.29.0
Thrusters
Attack Pattern

Large aerial platform that hovers at altitude and rains rockets onto player positions. Area-denial — punishes static cover hard. As of 1.29.0, multi-missile salvos are now spread over a larger area, preventing multiple missiles from hitting the same spot simultaneously.

Weak Points
Thrusters (require sustained damage)
Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher / Rascal grenade launcher Heavy ammo AR or LMG EMP Grenade (interrupt rockets, drop altitude)
Strategy Never stay in one spot. Keep moving laterally between cover pieces. Fire rockets into the thrusters during its attack cycle. EMP interrupts a salvo and drops it lower for easier shots. The 1.29.0 spread fix makes multi-missile salvos slightly safer to reposition through.
Vaporizer HP ~1,500–8,000 ⚠ More maps 1.26.0
Thrusters (no shield)
Attack Pattern

Added in Flashpoint (1.22.0). Large aerial ARC with a Matriarch-grade energy shield and precision lasers that destroy your shield and ignite you in under two seconds. Added to more maps and conditions in 1.26.0. Energy shield blocks all standard projectiles while active. A patrol movement bug was fixed in 1.26.0.

Weak Points
Thrusters (only when shield is down)
Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher (burst during shield break) Heavy ammo AR (thruster fire) EMP Grenade — CRITICAL (breaks shield) Fire-resistant trinket / armor
Strategy EMP grenades are mandatory — they break the energy shield. The instant the shield drops, dump everything into the thrusters. Short window before it reactivates. Stay mobile and use building angles to break laser tracking. Killing it drops the Vaporizer Regulator to craft the Dolabra shotgun.
Boss Boss Encounters
The Queen HP ~15,000–33,864 ⚠ Collision fix 1.29.0
Legs / Head / Core
Attack Pattern

Apex-class boss (Harvester event). Heavy mortar fire, precision laser beam, area EMP pulse, and a ground slam at close range. Missiles can obliterate an unprepared squad. As of 1.29.0, now more often steps over obstacles rather than through them, reducing unfair knockback. Aim assist correctly targets weak spots since 1.26.0.

Weak Points
Leg joints Head sensors Core (exposed in later phases)
Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher / Rascal (joints / core) Heavy ammo LMG (sustained leg fire) Full squad of 3 — split aggro EMP Grenades (interrupt laser / EMP pulse) Shield cells + med kits — bring plenty
Strategy Split the squad on opposite sides to split her aggro. Focus all fire on leg joints to strip sections. Shield up immediately when she charges the EMP pulse. Avoid melee range — the ground slam is a near-instant kill. She becomes more aggressive as HP drops.
Matriarch HP ~20,000+ ⚠ Collision fix 1.29.0
Legs / Head core
Attack Pattern

Deadliest boss. Spawns on Dam Battlegrounds, Blue Gate, and Spaceport only — never Stella Montis, Buried City, or Riven Tides. Projects an energy shield that blocks all projectiles. Fires rocket swarms, deploys gas mortars, uses flashbangs, and summons ARC reinforcements. Obstacle collision fixed in 1.29.0; aim assist targets weak spots correctly since 1.26.0.

Weak Points
Leg joints Head core (shield must be down)
Weapons & Items
Rocket launcher / Rascal (head core during shield break) Heavy ammo AR (leg joint sustained) EMP Grenade — CRITICAL (breaks shield) Gas Mask / Respirator (counters gas mortars) Assign one player to ARC adds Flashbang-resistant trinket
Strategy Assign one squad member to the summoned ARC adds — letting them build is a wipe condition. EMP to break the shield, then immediately dump rockets into the head core. Gas mask or keep moving through mortar clouds. Flashbang trinkets are essential. Highest coordination fight in the game.
ARC Turbine HP ~4,000 ⚠ Visual fixes 1.29.0
Yellow canisters
Attack Pattern

Airborne boss exclusive to Riven Tides (added 1.26.0). Massive cone-shaped aerial machine with two phases. Flying phase: nearly invulnerable — do not waste ammo. Launches a spike that splits into 3 lightning projectiles that penetrate walls. Deploys mines in a wide perimeter. Spawns after 15 minutes into a match; can appear in duos or trios. Ground phase: lands on three legs, opens its core to reveal three spinning yellow canisters — you have roughly 4–5 shots before it lifts off. Visual/positioning fixes and increased burn odds when inside it in 1.29.0.

Weak Points
Three yellow spinning canisters in landing gear (ground phase only)
Weapons & Items
Hullcracker (strong vs canisters) Jupiter / Anvil (multi-round, no reload gap) Renegade rifle (precise mid-range) Medium or Heavy Shield — essential Shield rechargers + med kits in quantity Avoid Wolfpack — ineffective vs Turbine armor
Strategy Do not fire while airborne — immune to damage, and you'll trigger mine/lightning attacks. The Turbine tends to land when it has not been attacked for a while — patience triggers the ground phase. The instant it sets down, all squad members fire simultaneously into the spinning yellow canisters through the landing gear slits. 4–5 shot window. After it lifts off, its split-lightning grenades penetrate walls — don't shelter inside buildings expecting cover. Drops Turbine Compressors for the Avian Alarm project and Powered Descender crafting.
